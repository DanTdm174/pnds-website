Meet the team

Flora Tu

Co-President

Flora Tu is a senior at Burnaby North Secondary School. She started debate in 8th grade at Vancouver Debate Academy. There, she debated under "VDA - Vancouver TZ" and ranked as the top 2025–2026 season international team in NDCA points and bid count. She was also tied for 17th in bid count in Public Forum in the world. Outside of debate, Flora likes to play piano and collect vinyl.

Notable awards

  • 8 career bids to Tournament of Champions
  • 2026 Florida Blue Key: 2nd Seed
  • 2025 Peach State: Quarterfinalist
  • 2024 Lower Mainland East: Champion + Top Speaker
  • 2024 Stanford Invitational: Semifinalist
  • 2021 Harvard National Tournament: Octafinalist

Angelina Guo

Co-President

Angelina Guo is a Junior at Hugh McRoberts Secondary. She has been involved with the Pacific Northwest Debate Society for 2 years where she teaches debate and organizes events. She competes in varsity Public Forum and has most notably received awards from Stanford, Harvard, and Berkeley. Outside of debate, Angelina serves as Co-President of Adopt Our Community, serves food insecure populations in the Lower Mainland, competes in international MOOT Court Competitions as an outstanding attorney, and is a Petty Officer First Class with RCSCC Fraser.

Notable awards

  • Ranked 8th internationally on the MSPF scoreboard among 2,000 teams
  • 2023 WSC Global Xiamen: Showcase Speaker (top 8 of 1,500)
  • 2024 Seattle University PF Tournament: 2x Champion (Spring and Fall)
  • 2024 Vancouver WSC: 3rd Place Team
  • 2024 Berkeley Invitational: Quarterfinalist

Marcus Zhang

Co-President & Coaching Director

As a junior at Magee Secondary School, Marcus is beyond ecstatic to serve as Co-President of PNDS for the 2026–27 term. He is committed to breaking down the pervasive barriers that prevent young and blossoming students from pursuing debate by providing free classes and tournament opportunities. He hopes that all new debaters will become enamoured with debate as he once was as a naive fourth grader, and that they are able to find their own voice within PNDS or debate in general. In his free time, he can be found stuck choosing which toppings to get on his Oakberry bowl, trying to understand (with much failure) whatever critical theory he has stumbled upon, or simply perusing the newest items within a grocery store.

Miguel De Las Alas

Director of Fundraising

Miguel is experienced in the formats of Lincoln-Douglas, Public Forum, the Canadian National Debate Format, and British Parliament. He is also well versed in Model UN. Outside of debate, Miguel is a 10th grade student at Richmond Christian School and plans to become a computational neuropathologist, leveraging computational tools to detect diseases. Currently, he is using deep learning to detect cognitive impairment in 3D volumetric brain scans, and has secured mentorship at the University of British Columbia. He also leads clubs at RCS that aim to inquire critically and to give back to the community.

Notable accomplishments

  • Founder and Lead Developer of FUNNEL, a 3D neural network research project on brain MRI, presented to UBC faculty
  • 2025 TEDxRCSYouth Speaker on neurodiversity
  • 2024 Greater Vancouver Regional Science Fair: Silver Medalist
  • SchoolReach: 3rd at Alberta Provincials (Junior), 4th at Nationals

Victor Chen

Coaching Director

Victor is a senior in Grandview Heights Secondary and has debated for 6 years with experience in CNDF, PF, Worlds, and BP. He leads his school's debate club as President, and serves as the coaching director of PNDS. Outside of the debate space, he loves to read, talk about philosophy, and play golf.

Notable awards

  • 2024 Stanford Invitational: Octafinalist and 8th Speaker
  • 2024 DTA: Octafinalist
  • 2024 Harvard International Round Robin Qualifier
  • 2023 Digital TOC #3: Octafinalist
  • 2023 Harvard National Tournament: Doubles
