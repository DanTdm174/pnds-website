Questions from parents

Are classes really free?

Yes. Our Fall Courses are free. Camps and tournaments are low-cost, and what we earn from events is reinvested in our programs.

What grades are classes for?

Grade 3 and up. Students of all experience levels are welcome, including complete beginners.

When and where are classes?

Every Tuesday from 4:00 to 5:30 pm Pacific Time, live on Zoom.

Who teaches the classes?

Our student executive team, led by our Coaching Director. Our coaches are experienced high-school debaters who compete in formats like Public Forum, CNDF, Worlds, and British Parliamentary. You can read their bios in Meet the team.

Can my child join partway through the term?

Yes. It's not too late to join. Sign up here and we'll get you set up.

What does my child need?

A computer or tablet with Zoom and a working microphone.

Is PNDS a registered organization?

Yes. Pacific Northwest Debate Society is a non-profit society registered in British Columbia (S0078788).

How do I contact you?

Email pnds.inquiry@gmail.com, message our WeChat (pnds_inquiry), or find us on Instagram.
