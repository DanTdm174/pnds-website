Miguel De Las Alas

Director of Fundraising

Miguel is experienced in Lincoln-Douglas, Public Forum, the Canadian National Debate Format, and British Parliamentary, and is also well versed in Model UN. Outside of debate, Miguel is a 10th grade student at Richmond Christian School and plans to become a computational neuropathologist, using computational tools to detect disease. He is currently using deep learning to detect cognitive impairment in 3D volumetric brain scans, with mentorship at the University of British Columbia. He also leads clubs at RCS that aim to inquire critically and give back to the community.

Victor Chen

Coaching Director

Victor is a senior at Grandview Heights Secondary and has debated for six years, with experience in CNDF, Public Forum, Worlds, and British Parliamentary. He leads his school's debate club as President and serves as the coaching director of PNDS. Outside of debate, he loves to read, talk about philosophy, and play golf.
