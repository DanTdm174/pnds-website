Pacific Northwest Debate Society

Debate for every student in BC and beyond.

PNDS is a student-led youth non-profit. We run tournaments and training so that more students can learn to debate in an environment that is accessible, supportive, and educational.

Join the committee Contact us

What we stand for

Our mission is to make debate open to every student, wherever they start from.

Accessible

Low-cost and online events mean cost and distance don't keep students out of debate.

Supportive

Run by students, for students. New debaters are welcome, and experienced debaters help them grow.

Educational

Each round is a chance to practise critical thinking, research, and public speaking.

Events

We host online tournaments that are open to debaters across the region and internationally.
