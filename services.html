Services

Camps, tournaments, and fundraisers for debaters in BC and beyond.

Seasonal debate camps

Short, focused camps that boost students' debating skills, and a chance to meet people who share the same passion for debate. All earnings from camps are donated.

Tournaments

Tournaments where debaters compete with each other, refine their skills, and dig into interesting topics. Our goal is an educational, interactive, and competitive debate platform.

Fundraisers

Fundraisers such as Krispy Kreme sales and busking increase what we can donate to other good causes, sometimes in collaboration with other non-profits.
