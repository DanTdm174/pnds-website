Our mission

An affordable, supportive, and educational environment for students.

Our mission in a nutshell

The Pacific Northwest Debate Society's (PNDS) sole purpose is to create an affordable, supportive, and educational environment for students. We value students' voices and are dedicated to giving them a powerful platform for expression. Effective implementation of these objectives requires attention to two vital aspects: community and accessibility.

Community

PNDS works to connect debate clubs and teams across BC and beyond, creating a platform to exchange ideas. Students who join the society get opportunities to train, compete, and connect with peers across the area through camps, tournaments, fundraiser events, and more.

Accessibility

As debate has grown in popularity, cost has become a real barrier. An in-person tournament can cost thousands of dollars, and debate tuition can run as high as $80 an hour. PNDS makes the debate community more inclusive by offering affordable resources that give aspiring debaters the skills they need. All profits go to charitable causes.
